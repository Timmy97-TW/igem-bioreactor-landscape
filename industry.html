01

The industry is walking toward this architecture產業正朝這套架構前進

Perfusion with cell retention stopped being exotic somewhere in the last decade. The share of facilities that will not even consider it has fallen from about a third to roughly one in nine. That is the one axis on which a student device and a commercial plant are doing the same thing. 細胞截留灌流在過去十年間已不再是冷門選項。連考慮都不考慮的廠區比例，已由約三分之一降至約九分之一。 這是學生裝置與量產廠唯一在做同一件事的面向。

And the regulator has already written it down而主管機關已將其明文化

ICH Q13 Annex III describes continuous upstream processing with cell retention — the architecture of this reactor — and seventeen continuous-manufacturing approvals were on record by May 2026. The thing ReLEAF built is not a curiosity. What ReLEAF has not got is any of the evidence that those approvals rest on. ICH Q13 附錄 III 已載明具細胞截留的連續上游製程——即本反應器的架構—— 截至 2026 年 5 月，已有十七項連續製造核准案在案。ReLEAF 所建構的並非奇想； ReLEAF 欠缺的，是那些核准案所仰賴的全部證據。

02

Five ways to keep the cells, and the one we chose留住細胞的五種方法，以及我們選的那一種

Every perfusion reactor needs a device that lets medium leave while biomass stays. The industry has five, and they are not interchangeable. The one we built has a known, published weakness — and the single most interesting number on this page is that our own device does not show it. 每一台灌流反應器都需要一個「讓培養基離開、讓生物量留下」的裝置。產業共有五種，彼此不可互換。 我們所建的這一種有一項已知且見諸文獻的弱點——而本頁最值得一看的數字，正是我方裝置並未出現該弱點。

What a 2% difference does and does not mean2% 的差異代表什麼、不代表什麼

The published weakness of hollow-fibre TFF is that the membrane can hold back the product as well as the cells — up to half of a monoclonal antibody in the study that measured it. A 37 kDa enzyme through a 0.2 µm pore is a far easier problem than a 150 kDa antibody, so finding almost no difference between the two sides is what the physics predicts. 中空纖維切向流已見諸文獻的弱點，是膜不僅截留細胞，也可能截留產物—— 在量測到此現象的研究中，單株抗體最高有一半被截留。37 kDa 的酵素通過 0.2 µm 孔徑， 遠比 150 kDa 抗體容易，因此兩側幾乎無差異，正是物理所預期的結果。

So this is not a result that beats industry. It is a result that confirms the module was chosen for the right duty, which is a smaller and more defensible claim, and the one we make. It rests on a single run with no replicate, and both numbers are extrapolated. 因此這並非勝過產業的成果，而是證實模組選型符合其任務的成果—— 一個更小、也更站得住的主張，亦即我們所採取的立場。該結論僅來自單次運轉、無重複，且兩個數值皆為外插值。

That is not a flaw; it is what makes the device buildable by a school team and cleanable by hand. But it fixes the ceiling. What a 0.2 µm barrier is, and what it is not0.2 µm 屏障是什麼，又不是什麼

The containment claim is the heart of this project, so it deserves the standard that governs it rather than our own enthusiasm. 圍阻主張是本專案的核心，因此它應當面對規範它的標準，而非我們自己的熱忱。

ASTM F838-20The qualifying test for a sterilising-grade 0.2 µm filter: Brevundimonas diminuta ATCC 19146 at 107 organisms per cm² of membrane.0.2 µm 滅菌級濾膜的認定試驗：Brevundimonas diminuta ATCC 19146，每平方公分膜 107 個菌體。
What the standard itself says標準本身的說明That other species may be more penetrating, that it gives no assurance of complete retention, and that it is not product- or process-specific validation.其他菌種可能更具穿透性，該試驗不保證完全截留，且不構成針對特定產品或製程的驗證。
So it covers因此涵蓋Vegetative bacteria of roughly that size. B. subtilis at 0.8 × 3 µm is comfortably inside.約略該尺寸的營養型細菌。枯草桿菌為 0.8 × 3 µm，明顯在範圍內。

07

On-farm production already exists. Not with an engineered organism.農場端生產早已存在，但不是用工程化菌株

The market ReLEAF aims at has a working commercial precedent, in Brazil, at a scale that dwarfs anything in synthetic biology. It is also the clearest statement of what the regulatory ceiling currently is. ReLEAF 所瞄準的市場，在巴西已有實際運作的商業先例，其規模遠超合成生物學領域的任何案例。 它同時也最清楚地呈現了目前的法規天花板所在。

SOLUBIO · BR

300+ on-farm biofactories300 座以上農場端生物工廠

Covering 2.5–2.7 million hectares on take-or-pay contracts of five years minimum, R$168.7 m net revenue. Neither tank volume nor unit price is published anywhere — the single most useful number missing from this page. 覆蓋 250–270 萬公頃，採至少五年期的照付不議合約，淨營收 1.687 億巴西幣。 槽體容積與單機價格均未公布——這是本頁最需要卻付之闕如的數字。

LEI 15.070/2024

Own use only, and no sale僅限自用，不得販售

Brazil's law permits on-farm production for the farm's own use, prohibits selling what you make, requires strains from an accredited collection and five years of records. The business model ReLEAF describes would have to fit inside that shape. 巴西法規允許農場為自用而生產，禁止販售自製產品，要求菌株須來自認證菌種庫並保存五年記錄。 ReLEAF 所描述的商業模式必須納入此一框架之內。

EMBRAPA

18 of 18 contaminated18 件中 18 件遭污染

Embrapa's survey of on-farm product found every sample contaminated and no Bradyrhizobium in six that claimed it. Quality control, not strain performance, is what the literature names as the obstacle. Embrapa 對農場端產品的調查發現所有樣品均遭污染，且六件宣稱含慢生根瘤菌者實際皆無。 文獻所指出的障礙是品質管制，而非菌株效能。

BIOSPHERE · US

US$9 m, container scale900 萬美元，貨櫃規模

A DEVCOM award in May 2026 for a container-scale point-of-use reactor. Specifications are not published, and this is on a secondary source only. 2026 年 5 月獲美國陸軍 DEVCOM 補助，發展貨櫃規模的使用點反應器。 規格未公布，且本條僅有二手來源。

The gap ReLEAF is actually standing inReLEAF 實際站立的空缺

There is no commercial precedent anywhere for on-farm production of an engineered microbe or a recombinant peptide. That is simultaneously the project's strongest reason to exist and the reason no regulatory path for it can be cited — because none has been written. 在農場端以工程化微生物或重組胜肽進行生產，全球並無商業先例。 這既是本專案最強的存在理由，也是無法引用任何法規途徑的原因——因為那條路根本還沒被寫出來。

But但是: at 0.02 m² of membrane it is the smallest working example of that architecture anyone has published. 以 0.02 m² 的膜面積而言，它是已公開的該架構實作中最小的一個。
IT IS AN INSTANCE OF屬於此一趨勢

Point-of-need manufacturing需求點製造

Making the product where it is used rather than shipping it, which is what the biostimulant industry's own restraint list — shelf life, cold chain, distribution, price — keeps pointing at. Note that the list never names strain efficacy. 在使用地點製造而非運送產品，正是生物刺激素產業自身的限制清單——保存期限、冷鏈、配送、價格—— 一再指向的方向。值得注意的是，該清單從未提及菌株效能。

But但是: SoluBio proved the model at 300 sites with ordinary organisms. The engineered half is still unprecedented and unregulated. SoluBio 已在 300 個場址以一般菌株證明此模式可行；工程化的那一半仍無先例、亦無法規。
IT IS NOT AN INSTANCE OF不屬於此一趨勢

Process intensification製程強化

Intensification is measured in grams per litre per day. ReLEAF has never published a volumetric productivity, has never counted cells per millilitre, and has never measured oxygen transfer. The word does not apply. 製程強化以每公升每日公克數衡量。ReLEAF 從未公布體積產率、從未計數每毫升細胞數、 也從未量測氧傳。這個詞並不適用。

But但是: a gassing-out test would fix the oxygen blank in an afternoon, and it is the cheapest number left on the table. 一次除氣法測試可在一個下午補上氧傳的空白，那是目前最容易取得的數字。
A WARNING FROM THE INDUSTRY來自產業的警訊

Throughput was never the constraint產能從來不是瓶頸

Cellares raised US$327 m on automated throughput and lost a US$380 m agreement in August 2026 on process equivalence — not on capacity. Building the vessel is the part that can be done; showing the process does the same thing every time is the part that cannot be skipped. Cellares 以自動化產能募得 3.27 億美元，卻於 2026 年 8 月因製程一致性——而非產能—— 失去一筆 3.8 億美元的合約。造出槽體是做得到的部分；證明製程每次結果一致，則是無法略過的部分。

Read as解讀為: the replication run ReLEAF never did is worth more than another prototype. ReLEAF 始終未做的重複運轉，其價值高於再造一台原型。

The honest sentence誠實的一句話

On working volume, documentation and price transparency, ReLEAF is in the same conversation as a commercial benchtop system. On closed control loops, oxygen, run duration, cell density and productivity, it is a student device and nowhere near. 在工作體積、文件完整度與價格透明度上，ReLEAF 與商用桌上型系統處於同一層級； 在閉合控制迴路、氧傳、運轉時數、細胞密度與產率上，它是一台學生裝置，差距甚遠。
