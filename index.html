00

What is actually ours我們真正獨有的部分

Nine claims, each counted against the thirty projects in this audit, and each carrying the sentence that says what it does not establish. Four of the nine are not in our favour. 九項主張，每一項都以本文所審的三十個專案為分母計數，並附上「它不能證明什麼」。 其中四項對我們不利。

The claim, and the evidence for each half of it主張本身，以及其兩半的證據

A containment barrier is easy to claim and hard to show, because showing it means measuring two opposite things on the same part. We have both numbers from the same running reactor. 圍阻屏障易於宣稱、難以證明，因為證明它必須在同一個零件上量到兩件相反的事。 這兩個數字我們都取自同一台運轉中的反應器。

Cells do not cross細胞不會通過 Shell-side medium plated twice, from two different runs. Neither plate grew a colony. The lumen plate beside one of them did. 殼側培養液取自兩次不同運轉、各平盤一次，兩盤皆未長出菌落；其中一次並列的管腔盤則長菌。
Product does cross產物會通過 ACC deaminase by ELISA in integrated run 2: 10.7 µg/mL in the lumen, 10.5 µg/mL on the shell side. DNPH found activity on both sides. Both values sit above the assay’s top standard, so both are extrapolated. 整合運轉 2 的 ELISA：管腔 10.7 µg/mL、殼側 10.5 µg/mL；DNPH 於兩側皆測得活性。兩個數值皆高於分析法最高標準品，故均為外插值。
It keeps crossing持續通過 Integrated run 4: above 2 µg/mL in the shell permeate through 72 h, active by DNPH from 18 h onward. 整合運轉 4：殼側透過液於 72 小時內維持 2 µg/mL 以上，自 18 小時起即具 DNPH 活性。
What this is not這不等於 Two platings are not a containment validation. No log reduction was measured, the host-strain control itself gave assay signal, and a 0.2 µm barrier retains vegetative cells — it is not a claim about DNA, phage, spores, or the 20–200 nm vesicles the team says pass. 兩次平盤不構成圍阻驗證。未量測對數削減值，宿主菌株對照本身亦產生分析訊號，且 0.2 µm 屏障截留的是營養細胞——並非對 DNA、噬菌體、芽孢，或團隊載明會通過的 20–200 nm 囊泡的主張。

01

Rulebooks規則

Two documents decide how this work is read: the Biomanufacturing village description and the four aspects of the Hardware special award. Both are quoted rather than paraphrased, because the whole audit below is scored against them. 有兩份文件決定這些工作如何被閱讀：生物製造村的說明，以及硬體特別獎的四項面向。此處照引原文而非改寫， 因為下方整份審查都依此評分。

Biomanufacturing village生物製造村

Teams in this village use engineered biological systems to produce materials, chemicals, fuels, foods and other products, and address the processes, scale and economics that production at useful volume requires. 本村隊伍運用工程化生物系統生產材料、化學品、燃料、食品及其他產品，並處理在實用規模下生產所需的 製程、放大與經濟性問題。

The operative words are process, scale and economics. A vessel that holds a culture answers none of the three on its own. 關鍵詞是製程、放大與經濟性。單憑一個能裝培養液的容器，三者皆未回答。

Hardware special award硬體特別獎

  1. Need addressed.需求解決。 What synthetic-biology problem does this solve that an off-the-shelf instrument does not? 本裝置解決了現成儀器無法解決的什麼合成生物學問題？
  2. User testing.使用者測試。 Someone outside the team used or reviewed it, and a design decision moved as a result. 團隊以外的人實際使用或審視過，並因此改變了某項設計決定。
  3. Utility proved by measurement.以量測證明實用性。 A measured number, and the requirement it has to beat. 一個實測數字，以及它必須勝過的需求值。
  4. Reproduction.可重現性。 Itemized bill of materials, dimensions or CAD, firmware, assembly manual, licence. 逐項物料表、尺寸或 CAD、韌體、組裝手冊、授權條款。

Aspect 3 is where this field falls down. Of the thirty projects here, twenty-two built something that ran, and three answer aspect 3 in full with a measured number against a stated requirement. 第 3 面向是這個領域最常失守之處。本表三十個專案中，二十二個做出確實運轉的實體，而只有三個以對照明確需求的實測數字 完整回答了第 3 面向。

08

The system整體系統

The ReLEAF bioreactor is not an entry in the vessel category. It is one stage of a chain that starts at a weather record and ends on a leaf, and the reason it is built the way it is lies in two stages that are usually separate: the component that contains the organism is the component that releases the product. ReLEAF 生物反應器並非「容器」類別下的一個項目。它是一條自氣象記錄起、至葉片止的鏈條中的一段； 而它之所以如此設計，關鍵在於兩個通常彼此分離的環節在此合而為一：圍阻生物體的元件，就是釋出產物的元件。

01Field sensing田間感測 Weather records, field sensors and a satellite land layer fold into a stress index. Computation only; it has never driven a light. 氣象記錄、田間感測器與衛星土地圖層彙整為逆境指數。純屬計算，從未驅動過光源。
02Decision決策 The index is projected forward and decides whether the green channel should be driven. 指數向前推估，並決定是否應驅動綠光通道。
03Light光訊號 520 nm green to switch on, 660 nm red to switch off, through CcaS/CcaR. No induction curve exists yet. 520 nm 綠光開啟、660 nm 紅光關閉，經由 CcaS/CcaR。目前尚無誘導曲線。
04Expression表現 A Csn signal peptide carries ACC deaminase out of the cell. Every number on record is constitutive pVeg in strain 168. Csn 訊號肽將 ACC 去胺酶送出細胞。現有所有數據皆為 168 菌株中組成型 pVeg 的結果。
05Lumen管腔 The culture circulates and is read in line, without opening the loop. 培養液循環並於管線中即時量測，無需開啟迴路。
06Membrane膜 0.2 µm mPES hollow fibre. Retains 1–2 µm cells, passes a 37.6 kDa enzyme. Barrier and release path at once. 0.2 µm mPES 中空纖維。截留 1–2 µm 細胞，通過 37.6 kDa 酵素。同時是屏障與釋出路徑。
07Delivery輸送 Shell-side stream to soil or the hydroponic line. Not yet run end to end. 殼側流體送往土壤或水耕管線。尚未完成全鏈運轉。

What the chain does not yet do此鏈尚未做到的事

No forecast has driven a light, and no light driven by a forecast has reached a culture. Stages 01 to 04 are joined on paper and separately true on the bench; the join itself is untested. Every growth and secretion number below is therefore a constitutive number, and the control loop is open. 尚無任何預報驅動過光源，也沒有任何由預報驅動的光抵達培養液。第 01 至 04 階段在紙上相連、 在實驗台上各自成立，但接點本身未經測試。因此下方所有生長與分泌數據皆為組成型條件下的數據，控制迴路仍為開迴路。

Who else here is a system rather than a vessel 本表中還有誰是系統，而非容器

Six of the thirty projects integrate more than one unit operation. Against them, the ReLEAF claim narrows to something smaller and checkable. 三十個專案中有六個整合了一個以上的單元操作。與它們相比，ReLEAF 的主張收斂為一個更小、且可查證的說法。

50 mL growth, 50 mL expression, 1 L fed-batch polymer extension, then a steel extruder and a roller. The only project here that carries its product to a finished solid. 50 mL 生長、50 mL 表現、1 L 饋料批次聚合物延長，接著是不鏽鋼擠出機與收捲輪。 本表中唯一將產物做到固態成品的專案。

NUS SINGAPORE-A 2018

A loop that actually closes真正閉合的迴路

500 mL chamber, pump and an OD-plus-fluorescence sensor in one recirculating loop. Blue light switches off when OD₆₀₀ passes 0.6 and back on when the reporter hits 900. This is the precedent ReLEAF does not yet match. 500 mL 培養槽、幫浦與 OD 加螢光感測器構成一個循環迴路。OD₆₀₀ 超過 0.6 時藍光關閉， 報告基因讀值達 900 時再度開啟。這正是 ReLEAF 目前尚未達到的先例。

CALGARY 2020

A reactor whose output is a design產出為設計本身的反應器

A lab airlift reactor built only to parameterise a field reactor that the end user assembles from local materials. The deliverable is the specification, not the vessel. 實驗室氣升反應器的唯一用途，是為終端使用者以當地材料自行組裝的田間反應器提供參數。 交付物是規格，而非容器。

RUM-UPRM 2022

Four stages, influent to tested water四段式，自進水至已檢測水

RDX degradation, then two formaldehyde and nitrite stages, then a water-testing station. A train, specified in full and built in part. RDX 降解，接著兩段甲醛與亞硝酸鹽處理，最後為水質檢測站。一條完整規格、部分建成的處理列車。

TORONTO 2023

Separation upstream of the culture培養之前的分離段

Hollow-fibre membrane, then oxidation, then the bioreactor, with the separation modelled to 95.37 % methane in the retentate. The membrane sits before the reactor, not inside it. 中空纖維膜、氧化段、再進入生物反應器；分離段經建模可使截留側達 95.37% 甲烷。 該膜位於反應器之前，而非其中。

CORNELL 2025

Two organisms in one vessel單一容器中的兩種生物

One reactor body in a liquid-culture and a photobioreactor variant, built so a heterotroph and a photoautotroph can be cultured together. 同一反應器本體提供液態培養與光生物反應器兩種型式，使異營菌與光自營菌可共同培養。

The claim, narrowed to what survives the comparison經比較後仍站得住的主張

ReLEAF is not the only system here, and it is not the first to put light inside the vessel. Two things in it are still unmatched across thirty projects: the barrier that contains the organism is the same component that delivers the product, so containment and release cannot be traded against each other; and the switch is meant to be thrown by a forecast of a condition in a field rather than by an operator or a threshold inside the reactor. The second of those is design intent, not a result. ReLEAF 並非本表中唯一的系統，也不是第一個把光置入容器內的專案。但在三十個專案中仍有兩點無人並列： 圍阻生物體的屏障與輸送產物的元件是同一個，因此圍阻與釋出無法彼此交換；以及開關的觸發來自對田間狀態的預報， 而非操作者或反應器內部的閾值。後者屬設計意圖，尚非成果。

Results on record現有成果

Operating point操作點

Green is measured on a bench. Amber is computed from geometry and has no measurement beside it. Red is a figure that contradicts how the device was actually run. Grey is not measured at all. 綠色為實驗台實測值；琥珀色為由幾何計算而得、旁無實測對照； 紅色為與裝置實際運轉方式相矛盾的數字；灰色則完全未量測。

The one number on this grid that undoes another本表中自我抵銷的那個數字

The fouling margin J/J_crit = 0.04 is computed against a design flux of 2 LMH. Runs 5, 6 and 7 drew 72, 36 and 51 LMH — at or above the device's own computed critical flux of 46 LMH, and eighteen to thirty-six times the design flux the margin rests on. The team's pages never reconcile the two, and the fouling they did observe, a permeability halving from 2,520 to 1,210 LMH/bar in one run, is consistent with the higher number rather than the lower one. The honest reading is that the margin describes an operating point the reactor has never run at. 積垢裕度 J/J_crit = 0.04 是以 2 LMH 的設計通量計算所得。運轉 5、6、7 實際抽取 72、36、51 LMH—— 達到或超過裝置自行計算的臨界通量 46 LMH，並為該裕度所依據之設計通量的十八至三十六倍。 團隊頁面從未調和兩者，而實際觀測到的積垢——單次運轉中透過率由 2,520 降至 1,210 LMH/bar—— 與較高的數字一致，而非較低者。誠實的讀法是：該裕度描述的是反應器從未實際運轉過的操作點。

  2. A forecast registered before the run that scored it.在驗證運轉之前先行登錄的預報。 On 1 October, before any WB800 reactor run existed, the team posted a growth rate of 0.2986 h⁻¹ with a 90 % interval of 0.2705–0.3300 and fixed the pass criterion in advance. Run 6 gave 0.286 ± 0.010 h⁻¹. The prediction could have failed in public. 10 月 1 日，在任何 WB800 反應器運轉存在之前，團隊即公布生長速率 0.2986 h⁻¹ 與 90% 區間 0.2705–0.3300，並事先固定通過判準。運轉 6 實測 0.286 ± 0.010 h⁻¹。該預測原本可能當眾失準。

What keeps us off the top rung仍讓我們無法登上最高一級的原因

  1. The optogenetic loop is not merely open — the light is not in the reactor.光遺傳迴路不只是開的——光根本不在反應器裡。 The specification lists a 3 m green LED strip for CcaS/CcaR. The team's own software page states the light is not built into the bioreactor, and no induction curve exists anywhere. NUS Singapore-A closed a light loop on a measurement of the culture in 2018. 規格列有 3 公尺綠光 LED 燈條供 CcaS/CcaR 使用。團隊自己的軟體頁面載明該光源並未裝入生物反應器， 且各處皆無誘導曲線。NUS Singapore-A 早在 2018 年便以培養液的量測閉合了光控迴路。
  2. One control loop closes, and it lasted a night.只有一個控制迴路閉合，且只撐了一晚。 pH below 6.8 doses KOH to 7.0 — 53 times on run 7 — until 1 M KOH corroded the feeding valve and four broke consecutively. pH still ranged 6.34 to 7.97, outside the team's own 6.5–7.5 window. Pressure is displayed, not acted on. Dissolved oxygen is not measured at all. pH 低於 6.8 即投加 KOH 至 7.0——運轉 7 共 53 次——直到 1 M KOH 腐蝕進料閥， 四只接連損壞。pH 仍介於 6.34 至 7.97，超出團隊自訂的 6.5–7.5 範圍。壓力僅顯示、不作動。 溶氧完全未量測。
  3. Every integrated result is a single run.每一項整合結果都只有單次運轉。 No replication run was ever done, and neither October run carried a 168 control, so the two strains have never been compared inside the reactor. Every ELISA value sits above the assay's top standard, so all are extrapolated — and the host-strain control gave signal too. 從未進行重複運轉，且兩次十月運轉皆無 168 對照，因此兩株菌從未在反應器內比較過。 每個 ELISA 數值皆高於分析法最高標準品，故全為外插值——且宿主菌株對照組同樣產生訊號。
  4. The wiki contradicts itself in sixteen places.wiki 有十六處自相矛盾。 The worst for a judge: the Development page says the June pump leak is the only loss of containment on record and that no leakage occurred in any later test. Two later leaks are described on the Hardware and Engineering pages of the same wiki. Also unreconciled: 11 fibres and 200 cm² against 8 fibres and 150 cm², used in parallel on one page, and two cost totals a factor of two apart. 對評審而言最嚴重者：開發頁稱六月的幫浦滲漏是唯一一次圍阻失效，且其後各次測試均無滲漏。 然而同一 wiki 的硬體頁與工程頁描述了其後的兩次滲漏。另有未調和者：11 根纖維／200 cm² 對 8 根纖維／150 cm²，於同一頁並用；以及兩個相差一倍的成本總額。
  5. Cost, now that we publish one.成本——既然我們已公布。 US$1,022.67 on the bioreactor page's own list, and it excludes the controller, its mainboard, the dosing pump and the stir bar. That is the highest figure of the eight published here, roughly three times Cornell's ARC and eight times ASU's turbidostat. 生物反應器頁自列清單為 1,022.67 美元，且未含控制器、其主板、投藥幫浦與攪拌子。 這是本表八個已公布價格中最高者，約為 Cornell ARC 的三倍、ASU 恆濁器的八倍。

How a verdict was reached判定方式

BUILT means a photograph or run data proves a physical device exists. DESIGNED means a drawing, CAD or a spec with no device behind it. A purchased vessel is not a built device — Jiangnan-China ran a commercial 5 L fermenter and NJTech-China automated sampling on one, and neither counts here, though NJTech's own three-box build is the most hardware-serious work in the village after the six below. Several wikis are JavaScript applications that serve the same shell on every path; those were read out of their script bundles. 「已建成」指有照片或運轉數據證明實體裝置存在；「僅設計」指有圖面、CAD 或規格而無實體。 採購來的槽體不算自建裝置——Jiangnan-China 使用商用 5 L 發酵槽，NJTech-China 則在商用槽上自動取樣，兩者均不計入， 但 NJTech 自建的三件式裝置，是本村落中僅次於下列六隊的硬體成果。部分 wiki 為 JavaScript 應用程式， 所有路徑回傳同一外殼，這類頁面改由其程式包讀取。
