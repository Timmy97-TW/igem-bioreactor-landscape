01 / The two rulebooks兩份規則

What we are actually being judged against我們實際被評判的標準

ReLeaf sits in the Biomanufacturing village and nominates Hardware as one of three special awards. Those two documents decide the reading. Everything below is an attempt to score the field against them.ReLeaf 屬生物製造村，並將硬體列為三項特別獎提名之一。這兩份文件決定了評判方式；以下所有分析，都是依此對整個場域進行評分。

Biomanufacturing village生物製造村

"What can biology be programmed to manufacture? Biological systems can be used to make products under conditions that were previously impossible. Hardware special award: the four aspects硬體特別獎：四項評分面向

  1. Does the hardware address a need or problem in synthetic biology?該硬體是否解決合成生物學中的實際需求或問題？
  2. Did the team conduct user testing and learn from user feedback?團隊是否進行使用者測試，並從回饋中學習？
  3. Did the team demonstrate utility and functionality in their hardware proof of concept?團隊是否在概念驗證中展示了硬體的實用性與功能性？
  4. Is the documentation sufficient to enable reproduction by other teams?文件是否足以讓其他團隊重現？

Judge Handbook: strong competitors show "utility, user testing, and easy reproducibility by those in the community."評審手冊：有力競爭者須展現「實用性、使用者測試，以及社群易於重現」。

The structural fact that shapes everything below.決定以下一切的結構性事實。 The four aspects are four checkable claims: a stated need, a named outside user whose feedback changed something, a measured performance number, and a release package. Every score on this page comes from those four questions, which is why a team can be brilliant on physics and still land low. 這四項面向是四項可查證的主張：明確的需求陳述、具名且確實改變了設計的外部使用者、實測的效能數字，以及可釋出的完整資料包。本頁所有評分均源自這四個問題——這也是為何一支在物理分析上出色的隊伍，仍可能落在低分區。

03 / Definition定義

The fourteen metrics that define a bioreactor project定義一個生物反應器專案的十四項指標

A bioreactor project is not defined by the vessel. It is defined by which of these fourteen numbers the team can state. Group A is physics, group B is evidence, group C is what the Hardware rubric reads. Teams lose the award in B and C, almost never in A.生物反應器專案並非由容器定義，而是由團隊能說出哪幾項數字定義。A 組是物理、B 組是證據、C 組是硬體評分表實際會看的內容。隊伍幾乎從不輸在 A 組，而是輸在 B 組與 C 組。

07 / The ladder階梯

Five tiers, and which rung each project stands on五個級距，以及各專案所處位置

Each tier adds exactly one requirement to the one below it, so a project's tier is a statement about what it can prove, not about how clever it is.每一級僅比下一級多要求一件事，因此級距說明的是「能證明什麼」，而非「構想多聰明」。

08 / Re-ranked重新評級

ReLeaf moves from tier 3 to tier 2, and the reason is a numberReLeaf 自第 3 級升至第 2 級，理由是一個數字

The earlier ranking put ReLeaf in tier 3 because the permeate flow was recorded as unchosen, which left operating flux and therefore the fouling margin empty. With the invariant explorer and the material price list in hand, that is no longer true. Q_p is set at 2.5 mL/min and every dependent invariant falls out of it. Below is the full operating point, computed from the explorer's own physics engine at its default settings.先前評級將 ReLeaf 列為第 3 級，因為透過流量被記為未選定，導致操作通量與積垢裕度皆為空白。有了不變量探索工具與物料價格表，情況已不同：Q_p 定為 2.5 mL/min，所有相依不變量隨之確定。以下為完整操作點，直接以該工具的物理引擎在預設值下計算得出。

The one caveat that decides tier 2 against tier 1.決定第 2 級與第 1 級之別的唯一但書。 Every number above is computed, not measured. Wall shear comes out of Hagen–Poiseuille given a set cross-flow; critical flux comes out of the van Reis–Zydney shear-induced-diffusion law given a literature cell radius. Cornell's 37 klux was read off a light meter. Until a flux–TMP sweep measures J_crit on our own feed and a gassing-out test measures k_La in our own vessel, aspect 3 scores as prediction rather than proof. That single distinction is the whole gap to tier 1, and both experiments are afternoon-length. 上述每個數字皆為計算值而非量測值。管壁剪切由設定的交叉流量經 Hagen–Poiseuille 得出；臨界通量由文獻細胞半徑經 van Reis–Zydney 剪切誘導擴散律得出。Cornell 的 37 klux 是用照度計讀出來的。在以通量–TMP 掃描於我們自己的料液上實測 J_crit、並以除氣法於我們自己的槽體實測 k_La 之前，第 3 面向只能算預測而非證明。這一項區別就是通往第 1 級的全部差距，而這兩個實驗各只需一個下午。

Where we are genuinely ahead我們確實領先之處

Nobody else in iGEM defends a scale-up transformiGEM 中無人為放大轉換提出辯護

Across all seventeen projects, exactly one states which dimensionless groups it holds, which it sacrifices, and why. Cornell quotes a lux margin; Stuttgart runs a CFD; Toronto solves a membrane BVP. None names an invariant. Our dossier fixes wall shear and path length, scales area at constant flux under the van Reis linear-scaling convention, and says out loud that whole-system geometric similarity and the θ/τ control ratio are the groups being given up.十七個專案中，只有一個明確說出自己固定哪些無因次群、犧牲哪些、以及理由。Cornell 引用照度餘裕、Stuttgart 做了 CFD、Toronto 解了膜的邊界值問題，但皆未指名任何不變量。我們的論述固定管壁剪切與流道長度，依 van Reis 線性放大慣例在定通量下擴增面積，並明言所犧牲的是整體幾何相似性與 θ/τ 控制比。 TFF_ScaleUp_Statement_and_Plan.md §1–§4 · van Reis 1997 · Field 1995 · van Reis & Zydney 2007

The fouling margin is now a number, and it is a comfortable one積垢裕度現已是數字，且相當寬裕

J/J_crit lands at 0.26 against a stated target of below 0.7. Wall shear at 5,305 s⁻¹ sits close to the 4,000 s⁻¹ operating point that van Reis and Zydney document for a 174 m² industrial hollow-fibre harvest, which is the sanity anchor our own plan asked for. No other iGEM project in this set publishes either quantity.J/J_crit 為 0.26，對比所設目標小於 0.7。管壁剪切 5,305 s⁻¹ 接近 van Reis 與 Zydney 所記載的 174 m² 工業級中空纖維收穫程序之 4,000 s⁻¹ 操作點——正是我們自己計畫所要求的合理性錨點。本表中無其他 iGEM 專案公布任一數值。 Explorer defaults: Q_xf 250 mL/min · Q_p 2.5 mL/min · X 2×10⁹/mL · V_w 400 mL

Perfusion with cell retention, in a field of batch jars在一片批次玻璃罐中，做細胞截留灌流

Two instruments and the reactor housing are ours兩件儀器與反應器外殼由我們自建

Two known operating faults, still unfixed兩項已知且未解的操作缺陷

There is no valve after the hollow fibre to build the pressure that pushes protectant out, and B. subtilis surfactant foaming is described by our own advisor as the real operating problem. Judges reward the team that names its failure modes. They do not reward the team that names them and arrives with the same device.中空纖維後方缺少建立壓力、將保護劑推出的閥門；而 B. subtilis 表面活性劑造成的起泡，被我們自己的顧問稱為真正的操作問題。評審獎勵指名自身失效模式的團隊，但不會獎勵指名之後、卻仍帶著同一台裝置出席的團隊。 張嘉修 · the missing valve · resting cells + cartridge proposed

The power budget does not survive scale-up電力預算撐不過放大

At one module the 45 W panel closes the day with 17 % to spare at winter irradiance. At ten modules the same panel delivers less than half the demand. Our own scaling plan flagged oxygen as the constraint that fails first; on these defaults power fails alongside it, and neither is fixed by adding membrane area.單模組時，45 W 面板在冬季日照下尚有 17% 餘裕；十模組時，同一面板僅能供應不到一半需求。我們自己的放大計畫已指出氧氣是最先失效的限制；在此預設值下電力與之同時失效，且兩者皆無法靠增加膜面積解決。 ×1 modules: 101 vs 86 Wh/day. ×10 modules: 101 vs 216 Wh/day.×1 模組：101 對 86 Wh/日。×10 模組：101 對 216 Wh/日。

The high-school read is different, and better.若以高中組來看，結論不同，而且更好。 The tiers are absolute across all three sections.
